# St. Flavius Catholic Church website

## Product and audience

This is the public website for St. Flavius Catholic Church, Oworonshoki. It serves parishioners, parish staff, ministry and society leaders, visitors, and people considering joining the parish. Its primary jobs are to communicate parish information clearly, help people participate in parish life, and provide trustworthy ways to contact the parish and report facility problems.

## Brand register

The interface uses a **brand** register: the parish should feel recognisable, reverent, welcoming, and dependable at every touchpoint.

## Visual personality

- Reverent and monumental, drawing from Gothic church architecture.
- Warm and welcoming rather than austere or theatrical.
- Architectural cues may include pointed arches, stone-like neutrals, fine tracery, restrained gold, and deep wine accents.
- Typography should be dignified and highly readable. Avoid blackletter display type.
- Avoid Halloween darkness, fantasy-medieval ornament, excessive decoration, or a cold museum atmosphere.

Notre-Dame is a directional reference for proportion, vertical rhythm, arches, and crafted detail—not a motif to copy literally.

## Content and interaction principles

- Use direct, pastoral language and explain what happens after a submission.
- Keep important parish actions easy to find on mobile and desktop.
- Make form progress, required fields, validation, and delivery status unambiguous.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve visible focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
